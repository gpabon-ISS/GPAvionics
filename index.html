FLY TO — Location Input Modes
The FLY TO box on the EFIS panel always accepts:
• Airport codes worldwide (ICAO or FAA), e.g. KSDF, 82IS, EDDK
• Coordinates in Google format, e.g. 42.1306, -88.3833
When the option above is enabled, it also accepts city names, ZIP/postal codes, and full addresses (e.g. Louisville, KY or 40202 or 1600 Pennsylvania Ave NW). Geocoding by OpenStreetMap.

Amateur Radio Satellite (SAT tab)
Pick a ham-radio satellite to track on the SAT tab of the widget. It shows the same live data and next-pass prediction as the ISS, calculated for your GPS location (or map center). TLEs from CelesTrak.

Map Layers
Adds bright place-name labels on top of the dark map so cities like Louisville, Chicago, Miami stand out. Only visible in dark mode.

Alert Location
By default, NWS alerts follow your GPS location (when GPS is on) or the center of the map. Move the map and alerts update automatically. To lock alerts to a specific NWS zone instead, enter a Zone ID below.
Find Zone ID by City or Address
Works for the USA, Alaska, Hawaii, and all U.S. territories (PR, VI, GU, AS, MP). Enter a city, address, or ZIP and we'll look up the matching NWS forecast zone.
NWS Alert Zone (optional override)
How to find your Zone ID manually:
1. Go to forecast.weather.gov
2. Search your city/ZIP. The URL and page footer show a zone like KYZ067.
Or browse the full list at weather.gov/gis/zones.

Example Zone IDs:
• KSDF Louisville → KYZ067
• KMIA Miami → FLZ173
• KPHL Philadelphia → PAZ106
• KBGR Bangor → MEZ030
• TJSJ San Juan, PR → PRZ008
• PANC Anchorage → AKZ101
• KORD Chicago O'Hare → ILZ014
• KDPA Chicago/DuPage → ILZ014
• KLGA New York LaGuardia → NYZ072
